Frequently Asked Electrical Questions

What areas in Southwest Florida do you provide electrical services?

We proudly serve Cape Coral, Fort Myers, Naples, Punta Gorda, and the surrounding communities in Lee and Collier counties.

Are your electricians fully licensed and insured in Florida?

Yes, Elite Electrical Contractors has been fully licensed and insured in the state of Florida since 2001 under license number EC13003980.

How does your upfront pricing and instant online booking work?

We allow you to view our service pricing directly on our website. Once you select your service, you can instantly book a time slot that works for you—no waiting for call-backs or surprise estimates.

Do you handle both residential and commercial electrical projects?

Absolutely. We have the scale and expertise to handle everything from home electrical repairs to large-scale commercial build-outs.

What does it mean that Elite Electrical Contractors is an employee-owned company?

We operate under an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP). This means our electricians are part-owners of the company, resulting in a higher level of pride, dedication, and quality in the work they provide to our customers.
