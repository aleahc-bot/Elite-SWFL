THE ONE SETUP STEP — THE RÉSUMÉ KEY
====================================================================
The upload box works the moment it is uploaded, but the email it sends
needs a mail service behind it. Fifteen minutes, free at this volume.

  1. Sign up at resend.com (free tier).
  2. Add the domain  elite1314.com  and follow their DNS steps, or use
     their test sender while you try it.
  3. Create an API key, copy it.
  4. In Vercel: your project > Settings > Environment Variables, add:

        RESEND_API_KEY    the key you just copied
        MAIL_FROM         website@elite1314.com
        MAIL_TO_CONTACT   jennifer@elite1314.com
        MAIL_TO_JOBS      jennifer@elite1314.com

  5. Redeploy.

The key lives in Vercel, never in the repo. That is deliberate — Vercel
serves .php as a readable download, so anything put inside send.php was
public. This cannot repeat that.

Until the key is set, the forms show "the site is not finished connecting
to its mail service yet" and give the phone number, rather than pretending
to send. Nothing is lost quietly.

MAIL_TO_CONTACT and MAIL_TO_JOBS are both jennifer@ for now. When Chipper
sets up the new addresses, change them in Vercel — no file edits, no
upload, no redeploy of the site itself.
